# My agent: Smart E-Commerce Concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps shoppers discover products, manage their cart, and get personalized recommendations with a catalog of items.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers shopper preferences, budget, past orders, and style choices.
- Tools: Product search, inventory lookup, cart management.
- Catalog/UI: Product catalog with price, stock, category, rendered as cards/tables.
- Image gen: Generates product visual previews or lifestyle images on demand.
- Sandbox: Cart totals, discounts, and tax calculations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: Vertex AI Memory Bank, Cloud Storage media uploads.
